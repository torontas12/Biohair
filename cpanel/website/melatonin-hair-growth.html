BIOHAIR X® INGREDIENT PROFILE

How this ingredient relates to BioHair X®

BioHair X® describes melatonin 0.5% as a component of its formulation. Published exploratory melatonin studies do not validate the 0.5% concentration or the full BioHair X® combination.

Hair follicle mechanism

An indoleamine studied for antioxidant signaling and hair follicle biology.

Clinical evidence and limitations

Small studies and preclinical research have investigated topical melatonin and hair-cycle outcomes. Larger, rigorous controlled trials are needed to define benefit, ideal concentration and patient selection.

Safety and prescribing considerations

Topical tolerability, formulation stability and possible systemic exposure require assessment; absence of strong adverse-event reporting is not proof of safety.
