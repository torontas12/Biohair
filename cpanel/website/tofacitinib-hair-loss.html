BIOHAIR X® INGREDIENT PROFILE

How this ingredient relates to BioHair X®

BioHair X® describes tofacitinib 3% as a component of its formulation. Systemic tofacitinib studies in alopecia areata do not demonstrate efficacy of a topical 3% combination for androgenetic alopecia or other hair-loss conditions.

Hair follicle mechanism

A Janus kinase inhibitor investigated for immune-mediated alopecia areata.

Clinical evidence and limitations

Clinical studies have reported regrowth in some alopecia areata patients receiving systemic JAK inhibition. Topical formulation data are substantially more limited; relapse after discontinuation and patient selection remain important considerations.

Safety and prescribing considerations

Systemic JAK inhibitors carry serious boxed warnings, including serious infections, malignancy, major cardiovascular events and thrombosis. Topical absorption and safety of this specific compounded preparation cannot be assumed.
