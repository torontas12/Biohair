03 / Formulation & Supporting Research

The Pharmacological Approach Behind BioHair X

Hair loss involves multiple biological pathways, from androgen-driven follicular miniaturization in androgenetic alopecia (AGA) to immune-mediated follicular disruption in alopecia areata (AA). BioHair X® brings together four pharmacologically active ingredients investigated for their roles in hair growth-cycle regulation, JAK signaling, androgen metabolism, and follicular oxidative stress.

Bimatoprost 1%

Prostamide Signaling · Anagen-Phase Regulation · Hair Follicle Growth

Bimatoprost is the active ingredient in FDA-approved LATISSE® 0.03%, which promotes longer, thicker, and darker eyelashes. It acts on prostamide signaling and may prolong the anagen (growth) phase of hair follicles. Research also suggests effects on hair bulb size and shaft thickness, providing a rationale for investigating scalp applications.

Multiple published studies have documented bimatoprost's hair-growth activity, including research involving higher-concentration scalp formulations. A 2013 study in The FASEB Journal demonstrated stimulation of human scalp hair follicles, while a 2026 clinical study of topical bimatoprost 1% confirmed scalp tissue penetration and favorable short-term tolerability in men with androgenetic alopecia (AGA). Building on this body of scientific and clinical evidence, BioHair X® incorporates bimatoprost 1% as a key component of its physician-directed topical hair restoration formulation.

Finasteride + Melatonin

DHT Regulation · Hair-Cycle Biology · Antioxidant Protection

Finasteride

Finasteride inhibits 5-alpha-reductase, reducing formation of dihydrotestosterone (DHT), a key contributor to follicular miniaturization in androgenetic alopecia (AGA). Its inclusion provides an androgen-targeted component within the multi-pathway formulation.

Topical finasteride has been studied as a scalp-directed treatment approach. BioHair X® includes finasteride 0.2% among its physician-customizable ingredients; absorption and outcomes can vary by formulation, and studies of other preparations do not validate the complete combination.

Melatonin

Melatonin receptors (MT1 and MT2) are present in human hair follicles. Research suggests that melatonin may influence the anagen growth phase and help protect follicles from oxidative stress and inflammation. These properties have prompted clinical investigation of topical melatonin in hair thinning.

In a randomized, placebo-controlled study involving 40 women, topical melatonin 0.1% used for six months improved the proportion of growing hairs in certain scalp regions. BioHair X® incorporates melatonin 0.5% to address follicular signaling and oxidative stress, but controlled trials have not established the efficacy of this concentration or the finished combination.

Tofacitinib 3%

JAK Signaling · Immune Pathways · Alopecia Areata Research

Tofacitinib inhibits Janus kinase (JAK) signaling, an immune pathway implicated in alopecia areata (AA). Oral JAK inhibitor studies have shown hair regrowth in some patients, supporting the pathway as a therapeutic target. This has encouraged investigation of topical JAK inhibition for localized treatment.

Small studies have also explored topical tofacitinib for AA. BioHair X® incorporates tofacitinib 3% as a physician-directed option, although results from oral therapy or other topical concentrations cannot establish the effectiveness or safety of this specific formulation.

06 / Physician FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BioHair X®?

A physician-directed, customizable topical formulation incorporating bimatoprost, tofacitinib, finasteride, and melatonin.

Can physicians customize the formulation?

Yes. Physicians can select active ingredients and concentrations through the compounding pharmacy.

Which types of hair loss are relevant?

Individual ingredients have been studied in androgenetic alopecia, alopecia areata, and diffuse thinning. The complete BioHair X® combination has not been proven effective for all these conditions.

Is BioHair X® FDA-approved?

No. BioHair X® is a compounded formulation, not an FDA-approved finished drug. Certain individual ingredients have FDA approvals for other uses.

Is clinical research available?

Yes. Studies support research into individual ingredients, but the finished BioHair X® combination has not been validated in controlled clinical trials.
