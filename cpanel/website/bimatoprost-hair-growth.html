BIOHAIR X® INGREDIENT PROFILE

How this ingredient relates to BioHair X®

BioHair X® describes bimatoprost 1% as one component of its formulation. FDA-approved bimatoprost ophthalmic solution for eyelash hypotrichosis uses 0.03%; that evidence does not establish the efficacy or safety of a 1% scalp preparation.

Hair follicle mechanism

A prostaglandin F2α analog with an established role in eyelash growth; scalp hair research is a separate question.

Clinical evidence and limitations

Mechanistic and small clinical studies have explored prostaglandin signaling and scalp hair. Concentration, delivery vehicle, population and outcomes matter; there is no established evidence here that the BioHair X® four-ingredient combination is clinically effective.

Safety and prescribing considerations

Potential adverse effects of bimatoprost products include irritation, pigmentation changes and unwanted hair growth; ophthalmic exposure raises additional concerns. Clinicians should evaluate the actual preparation and exposure route.
