Topical Finasteride for Hair Loss: DHT and Follicles

A 5α-reductase inhibitor used to reduce DHT signaling involved in androgenetic alopecia.

BIOHAIR X® INGREDIENT PROFILE

How this ingredient relates to BioHair X®

BioHair X® describes finasteride 0.2% as a component of its formulation. Evidence for oral finasteride and particular topical finasteride products cannot automatically be extended to this compounded multi-ingredient product.

Hair follicle mechanism

A 5α-reductase inhibitor used to reduce DHT signaling involved in androgenetic alopecia.

Clinical evidence and limitations

Clinical studies support finasteride for certain patients with androgenetic alopecia. Some topical finasteride formulations have been studied, but systemic absorption and efficacy depend on formulation, dose and use.

Safety and prescribing considerations

Finasteride can cause sexual and other adverse effects; it is contraindicated in pregnancy due to risk to a male fetus. Clinicians should evaluate reproductive risks, systemic exposure and counseling needs.
