## Review status

Seven responsive design families cover nine primary URLs, including three full HTML articles with original PDF downloads and a clinical library. Browser checks covered desktop, tablet and mobile; interaction checks passed, and automated accessibility checks reported no violations on the primary pages. The reports state the scope and limits of those checks.

This is a **staging review build**, intentionally blocked from indexing. It is not approved for public launch. See [pending items](downloads/PENDING_ITEMS.md), including domain confirmation, FAQ copy, service destinations, medical/rights approvals and live external-link verification. Forms and booking are not represented as configured services.

No production deployment or publication is authorized by these files. Do not replace a live cPanel document root without resolving pending items, backing up the current site and obtaining owner authorization for the final build.
